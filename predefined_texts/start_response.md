Greetings! This is GeroChat — a ChatGPT-like bot that can help you control your aging and well-being. All my answers are based on gerontology-related publications of [Alexey Moskalev](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Alexey-Moskalev) and his colleagues. 

You are free to think of your own prompts, but here are several recommendations to get the best results:

- *Be as specific as you can.*

  (Somewhat) bad example: "_What is glutathione?_"
  Good example: "_Explain glutathione: what it is, what it influences and which products restore it._"

- *If you want the answer to be presented in a specific form, just ask the bot to present them as such.*

  Example: "_Provide a list of intermittent fasting benefits_."

The "somewhat bad" examples will, of course, yield an adequate answer, but it's very likely to be short and not too informative.
